- **MCP Extensibility**: Existing suno-mcp-server. Studio/router ready for more (ComfyUI-MCP patterns emerging 2026). "USB-C for AI" connector layer.
- **6-Pillar Governance Baked In**: Via GenCreator CoE (Strategy/Governance/Talent/Technology/Data/Ethics). Ethics/attribution/lineage/royalties roadmap. Quality gates, visual doctrine (Atlantean Teal/Gold/Deep Void), TASTE/DESIGN standards.
